# Restaurant Wait Time & Customer Satisfaction Analysis

This project analyzes the operational factors influencing customer satisfaction in a restaurant environment, with a focus on wait times, table turn efficiency, staffing pressure, and promotional activity.

## Business Problem

The objective of this analysis was to identify the operational conditions most strongly associated with declining customer satisfaction and to develop actionable recommendations to improve service performance.

## Key Findings

- Customer satisfaction declined more rapidly when wait times exceeded approximately 20 minutes.
- Longer table turn times were associated with lower customer satisfaction and increased complaints.
- Promotional activity was concentrated during already busy periods, particularly Sunday and dinner service.
- Operational stress increased when guest volume exceeded approximately 15 guests per kitchen staff member and 7 guests per server.
- Higher guest counts increased revenue, but average ticket values and customer satisfaction declined as operational strain increased.

## Recommendations

1. Maintain average wait times below 20 minutes.
2. Reduce table turn times through service and kitchen process improvements.
3. Shift promotional activity toward lower-demand periods such as lunch.
4. Monitor operational capacity thresholds.
5. Focus on higher-quality revenue rather than simply maximizing traffic.
